Who I am

I’m Dragos.

I live in Zurich. I’ve been on this path for years, and I’m still on it. I’m not teaching it from the finish line.

It started with something I hated about myself. I was shy. I wanted to talk to people, and I didn’t. On the train I’d wait 20 minutes before saying something to someone. My heart was racing and I was shaking before I’d said a word. Most of the time I said nothing, and I was angry at myself the whole day.

What got me out was simple. Count 3-2-1 and say the first thing that comes to mind, even shaking, even afraid.

I used to be skinny. I built a body I’m proud of, and when I look in the mirror, that gives me confidence. It carried into every other skill I worked on: controlling my emotions, discipline, understanding myself.

I’m self-taught. Books and content, applied on myself. I followed the advice too. That’s how I know what advice can do for you, and what it can’t.

I also learned to watch my own thoughts. I notice the thought, I ask why I reacted the way I did, and I correct it. It’s called metacognition, and it’s one of the most powerful skills a man can learn.

A long-distance relationship of more than 2 years taught me a lot. And I have many female friends who’ve told me their side of dating and relationships. Their stories help me understand how men come across to women. I use them for that, never to tell you what “women want.”

I coach in 6 languages: English, German, Romanian, French, Italian and Spanish.
