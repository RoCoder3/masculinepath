Who I am

I’m Dragoș.
I was the nice guy.

When my ex did something that upset me and I said so, she got mad at my tone. And I was the one who apologised. Every time.
I kept asking myself: why do I always have to apologise? I wasn’t wrong.

I did the same to myself. I planned big and never kept my word. Why can’t I just be disciplined? It’s not that hard. For years I watched self-improvement and masculinity content, and I never acted on any of it.

I said yes when I meant no. I overthought what everyone would think of me. I couldn’t stand a few seconds of silence. Maybe you know this feeling.

It cost me my self-respect, years of trying to change, and the chance to live with less fear and less “I’m not worth it.”

Then I read No More Mr. Nice Guy by Robert Glover. I saw myself in it. It made me think about who I really was, and who I could be.

What got me out was small. The first times I texted no, I waited before sending it and rewrote it to sound softer. Then I sent “No, I won’t be able to come.” and nothing more. Nothing happened. I learned I can stay silent and nothing happens. I started doing things calmly, taking my time.

Now I plan something and I do it. I talk to strangers without overthinking it. I say and do what I want, and I still respect others. Women are drawn to me now, and dating goes well.

That’s why I coach men who are where I was years ago. I know how much being the nice guy makes you suffer, and I want to turn nice guys into masculine men. I live in Zurich and coach online or in person, in English, German, Romanian, French, Italian or Spanish. Wherever you are, you can do this in your own language.
